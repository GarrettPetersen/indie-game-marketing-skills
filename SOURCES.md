# Source ledger

Last reviewed: 2026-09-06

This repository summarizes and operationalizes the sources below. It does not
reproduce their wording or substitute for their complete work.

## BiteMe Games

- [Everything you need to make a great Steam page](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cYvV_RLwKJ8)
  — capsule, copy, trailer, screenshot, social, and localization review areas.
- [A Comprehensive Steam Demo Checklist](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=k9IixsdNP_g)
  — gameplay, onboarding, feel, audio, settings, and marketing readiness.
- [Ultimate Indie Gamedev Genre Tierlist](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Yg8wig1LTtE)
  — genre and production-risk discussion for small developers.
- [Small games win big, here's how to make them](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8Kr5TruT-YU)
  — scope discipline and designing within a small team's constraints.
- [Hyper-actionable steps to level up your game](https://www.gamesinprogress.com/indie-game-developers/biteme-games/hyper-actionable-steps-to-level-up-your-game)
  — creator research, playtesting, store iteration, demo definition, UI, and
  trailer work as concrete development tasks.

These are practitioner sources. Treat their recommendations as experienced
judgment and case evidence, not platform rules.

## How To Market A Game

- [How To Market Your Indie Game: A 10 Step Plan](https://howtomarketagame.com/2021/07/12/how-to-market-your-indie-game-a-10-step-plan/)
  — end-to-end framework beginning with genre and audience fit.
- [What genres are popular on Steam](https://howtomarketagame.com/2022/04/18/what-genres-are-popular-on-steam-in-2022/)
  — genre choice as a major commercial decision.
- [More evidence of which genres Steam shoppers love to play](https://howtomarketagame.com/2022/05/30/more-evidence-of-which-genres-steam-shoppers-love-to-play/)
  — corroborating festival and release evidence favouring deep builder,
  simulation, strategy, and RPG play over puzzle platformers.
- [Crafty Buildy Strategy Simulation Buyers Guide](https://howtomarketagame.com/2025/07/02/crafty-buildy-strategy-simulation-buyers-guide/)
  — a current practitioner account of the PC audience for buildy, systemic,
  replayable games.
- [Respect players' time by making longer games](https://howtomarketagame.com/2023/05/30/respect-players-time-by-making-longer-games/)
  — content leverage and playtime as part of small-team scope economics.
- [When should I post my Steam coming-soon page?](https://howtomarketagame.com/2025/03/10/when-should-i-post-my-steam-coming-soon-page/)
  — readiness gates for genre, art direction, environments, and gameplay media.
- [How do you promote a game before Steam Next Fest?](https://howtomarketagame.com/2024/11/13/how-do-you-promote-a-game-before-steam-next-fest/)
  — testing the demo before treating Next Fest as a major beat.
- [Making sense of the February 2026 Steam Next Fest](https://howtomarketagame.com/2026/04/13/making-sense-of-the-february-2026-steam-next-fest/)
  — current evidence on established wishlist base, recent momentum, and demo
  timing.
- [Games that used momentum for Steam Next Fest success](https://howtomarketagame.com/2026/07/14/games-that-used-momentum-for-steam-next-fest-success/)
  — distinguishes the lower-risk early-demo path from a difficult coordinated
  momentum launch.
- [Steam page checklist](https://howtomarketagame.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/03/SteamPageChecklistv1.pdf)
  — gameplay-first media and subgenre-first tags.
- [60 Marketing Mistakes](https://howtomarketagame.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/Zukowski_60MistakesEbookV1.pdf)
  — includes the failure mode of quietly publishing a coming-soon page instead
  of treating the game announcement as a coordinated press beat, and recommends
  making reusable capsule layers and logos easy for creators to download.
- [What Steam's big demo update means for your marketing strategy](https://howtomarketagame.com/2024/07/31/what-steams-big-demo-update-means-for-your-marketing-strategy/)
  — spacing announcement and demo beats, with exceptions for unusually strong
  coordinated showcases.

These sources mix observation, survey data, case studies, and author opinion.
Record which kind supports a recommendation rather than presenting every
heuristic as settled fact.

## Steamworks documentation

- [Graphical assets overview](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/store/assets)
- [Graphical asset rules](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/store/assets/rules)
- [Demos](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/store/application/demos)
- [Wishlists](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/marketing/wishlist)
- [Steam Next Fest](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/marketing/upcoming_events/nextfest)
- [User Reviews](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/store/reviews)
- [Get user reviews](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/store/getreviews)

Steamworks documentation is authoritative for Steam requirements but changes
over time. Retrieve the current page before asserting sizes, deadlines,
cooldowns, eligibility, notifications, or review lead times.

Steam review volume and text are useful opportunity-research inputs, but they
do not directly report unit sales. Review acquisition source, off-topic
activity, recency, and the actual causes of dissatisfaction must be considered
before treating a Mixed game as evidence of an addressable market gap.

## itch.io documentation

- [Uploading HTML5 games](https://itch.io/docs/creators/html5)
  — ZIP structure, relative paths, embed dimensions, fullscreen, and browser
  delivery considerations.
- [Controlling who can access your project](https://itch.io/docs/creators/access-control)
  — draft, restricted, and public access states plus unlisted distribution.
- [Limited releases](https://itch.io/docs/creators/limited-releases)
  — limited access and download-key workflows for bounded distribution.
- [Quality guidelines](https://itch.io/docs/creators/quality-guidelines)
  — page quality and presentation guidance.

itch.io is both a low-friction playtest host and a public storefront. The plan
must distinguish a restricted test from a public demo beat and verify current
access and packaging behavior before distribution.

## Web diagnostics and security

- [OWASP Logging Cheat Sheet](https://cheatsheetseries.owasp.org/cheatsheets/Logging_Cheat_Sheet.html)
  — event logging, sensitive-data exclusion, validation, transport, retention,
  and operational review.
- [GoogleChrome/web-vitals](https://github.com/GoogleChrome/web-vitals)
  — browser performance measurement primitives and attribution builds.

Telemetry law and consent requirements vary by jurisdiction and data collected.
The skill therefore requires explicit opt-in and data minimization but does not
pretend to replace legal review.
