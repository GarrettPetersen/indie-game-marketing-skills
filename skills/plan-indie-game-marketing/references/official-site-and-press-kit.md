# Official game site and downloadable press kit

Prepare the official site before the announcement beat so every piece of
coverage, creator email, social profile, festival form, and storefront has one
durable canonical destination.

## Site responsibilities

The site should quickly provide:

- the exact human-written pitch and game facts;
- authentic current gameplay media;
- the primary call to action, normally the Steam page or playable demo;
- supported platforms, languages, release status, developer, and publisher;
- press contact and official social profiles;
- press coverage and awards stated without exaggeration; and
- an obvious route to the press kit.

Use a memorable owned domain, HTTPS, responsive layout, accessible navigation,
descriptive metadata, and stable URLs. Test mobile and desktop layouts, all
downloads, social previews, language switching, and logged-out destinations.
Keep secrets, private builds, embargoed material, and personal data outside the
public deployment.

The site may point to a Steam demo, public itch.io demo, or access-controlled
browser playtest. Label each destination and its availability accurately; do
not expose a private build merely to make the press kit feel complete.

Every public marketing sentence, image description, metadata description,
biography, quote, and call to action must be supplied or approved as exact
human-written text. The agent may build and maintain the site around it but may
not fill copy gaps with generated prose. The lifecycle skill's narrow exception
permits factual privacy, consent, and telemetry boilerplate, subject to its
inventory, approval, and accuracy requirements.

## Press-kit contents

Provide files individually and in clearly named ZIP archives so a journalist or
creator can obtain only what they need. Include, when available:

- a concise factsheet using exact human-written descriptions;
- release status, platforms, languages, genre, price, and contact details;
- high-resolution screenshots from the current build without invented UI;
- public trailer embeds plus downloadable high-quality video files;
- transparent game logo and developer or publisher logo;
- human-made capsule and key art, including useful separated layers when the
  artist's license permits redistribution;
- portraits, character art, or other approved promotional assets;
- team biography and credits supplied by the developer;
- links to the store, demo, website, social profiles, and notable coverage; and
- asset credits, usage notes, and any embargo or attribution requirements.

Never place credentials, creator-contact lists, private application answers,
unreleased builds, licensed source assets that cannot be redistributed, or
personal information in the archive.

## Asset integrity

Keep one authoritative source for each asset and derive exports from it. Do not
stretch or silently change aspect ratios. Retain original-resolution downloads,
use predictable filenames, include language codes on localized variants, and
version ZIPs when their contents change materially.

Capsule art and key art must be made by the developer or a human artist. Agents
may validate dimensions, crop, resize, export, compress, package, and compose
supplied human-made layers, but may not generate the artwork.

## Release and maintenance gate

Before publishing or announcing:

- test every page and file from a logged-out browser;
- confirm the store and demo destinations are public when claimed;
- verify file sizes, formats, checksums where useful, and archive contents;
- confirm media and facts match the current build;
- remove drafts and private metadata from downloadable files; and
- record the deployment, source revision, and date checked.

Update the site and kit as part of every major marketing beat. Preserve stable
public URLs or redirects so old coverage does not rot.

Publishing or replacing the live site requires explicit authorization.
