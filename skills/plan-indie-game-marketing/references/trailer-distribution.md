# Send trailers to channels that post game trailers

Make trailer-hosting outreach a separate task for the announcement, demo,
release-date, and launch beats. These outlets can publish the developer's
finished video without playing the game or producing a review. Do not limit
the outreach list to streamers, wait for review keys, or assume a press release
automatically reaches their video editors.

## Choose distribution or a negotiated premiere

**Non-exclusive hosting is the low-friction default.** A trailer already on
Steam or the developer's YouTube channel can still be worth submitting. Tell
the outlet its actual publication status and provide the developer's exact
permission to download and repost it. Non-exclusive hosting is not second-rate
coverage, but neither is it an endorsement or a review.

**An exclusive premiere is an optional, time-limited trade.** Offer it when a
specific outlet's audience and proposed placement justify coordinating the
beat. Agree on the exact video/version, channels and territories covered,
publication time and timezone, when other media can upload, and what happens
if the outlet declines or misses the agreed slot. A YouTube scheduled Premiere
is a player feature, not itself an exclusivity agreement. An embargo sets a
not-before time; it does not by itself grant one outlet exclusive hosting.

**Keep a gameplay trailer on the Steam page.** The operating position is that
press/YouTube exclusivity excludes the Steam storefront, so the developer can
publish the same trailer there alongside the reveal. Obtain that carve-out in
writing rather than leave it implicit. Garrett reports that this is usually
how these arrangements work; that is practitioner experience, not a universal
IGN policy. Also clarify the game's website, developer YouTube, social clips,
embeds, and localized versions, rather than treating all owned channels as
automatically exempt. If broader terms conflict with having a useful Steam
page, negotiate narrower terms or choose non-exclusive distribution. Do not
strip the store trailer or delay the store page while waiting for a reply.

If a new video truly needs to remain unpublished during a pitch, keep the
existing gameplay trailer on Steam until the agreed reveal; do not commission
an extra teaser solely to accommodate an unconfirmed exclusive. Disclose any
already-public version before offering a first look. Never promise competing
outlets the same exclusive. No reply is not an agreement: set a bounded
decision date before the beat and proceed without a hold when no commitment
was made. Resolve any accepted obligation before changing the schedule.

Distinguish trailer hosts from gameplay creators in the contact log. The
gameplay-creator multi-game eligibility check is not a demand that a trailer
editor personally play two games; assess the outlet's recent relevant trailer
coverage instead. A build/key is optional unless requested or needed for a
separate review. Do not spend a limited key merely to deliver an MP4.

## Prepare one easy-to-use package

- A preview link, unlisted only while the agreed timing requires it, plus a
  direct high-quality MP4 download. Avoid making editors rip YouTube footage,
  request Drive access, or receive a huge email attachment. Test downloads
  logged out; check the outlet's current delivery specifications.
- The live Steam URL, accurate release/demo status, and the exact human-written
  short description and news. Provide official localized destinations when
  approaching regional channels; translation may faithfully preserve approved
  source copy under the skill's localization exception.
- A press-kit URL, correctly proportioned thumbnail options, a separate logo,
  and current screenshots. Reuse licensed supplied art; do not generate key art.
- Exact human-approved permission covering downloading, uploading and any
  requested edits/social excerpts, plus the agreed embargo/exclusive scope or
  clear non-exclusive status. Ask the human for those words rather than invent
  a rights grant. Do not request reposts of music or assets the developer cannot
  authorize; disclose known Content ID issues and provide a cleared version
  when needed.

Keep the request short and directed to **posting the trailer**, not an implied
obligation to play, review or endorse the game. Ask for the Steam link in the
description and the official social tag using human-supplied wording. Do not
offer payment, exclusivity, broad editing rights or a new embargo without the
developer's approval. Research and preparation do not authorize sending.

## Sequence and measure

For an agreed premiere, give that outlet its negotiated window, then distribute
to other suitable hosts when it ends. Otherwise, send the approved
non-exclusive package to a small relevant set around the beat without waiting
indefinitely for IGN. One useful follow-up or a genuinely new trailer beats
repeated identical pitches. Follow each outlet's stated rules.

Record outlet/contact, source checked date, trailer version, permission,
publication/embargo times, exclusive scope and Steam exception, submission,
reply, publication URL, and follow-up. Use per-outlet Steam UTM links when the
outlet will retain them. Track store traffic and wishlist/sales changes as
well as views; overlapping coverage makes attribution uncertain. Preserve the
result as social proof without implying a positive review.

Project case: on October 27, 2026, Indie Games Hub's M&R upload showed about
5.2k views roughly ten hours after publication. It included a Steam link and
the November 18 release date. This is a dated exposure observation, not a
measurement of incremental wishlists or a forecast for other games.

  in the register above. They do not establish a shared exclusivity policy.
