# Social and community distribution

Treat social posting as distribution to identifiable audiences, not a duty to
feed every platform. Prefer a few routes that have demonstrated fit over a
large undifferentiated content calendar.

## Classify the route before posting

- **Player audience:** people plausibly interested in buying or playing this
  kind of game.
- **Discovery gatekeeper:** a curator, account, community, or channel that
  actively looks for games to feature. A developer-heavy audience can still be
  valuable when a real gatekeeper watches it.
- **Peer feedback:** useful for development, but not automatically marketing.
- **Owned audience:** Steam followers, wishlisters, mailing-list readers, and
  existing community members who already expressed interest.

Do not mistake impressions among other developers for player demand. Record
what role each venue serves and what action the post should produce.

## Maintain a living opportunity register

For each recurring hashtag, subreddit, Discord channel, submission route, or
curator community, record:

- exact venue and channel;
- audience and why the game fits;
- allowed post type, links, developer disclosure, cadence, and cooldown;
- best available asset shape and call to action;
- official or first-party source;
- date the rules and channel name were verified;
- last post, next eligible date, UTM link, and result.

Named community routes are volatile. Recheck the channel and its rules on the
day of posting. A deleted channel, expired invite, changed moderator policy, or
new cooldown must make the calendar item fail visibly instead of falling back
to a general channel or posting anyway.

## Concrete opportunities

### Indie Game Joe Discord

As verified September 7, 2026, Indie Game Joe says the **show off your game**
channel in his Discord is the best place to share a game for his discovery
process. This is a discovery-gatekeeper route, not evidence that a general
developer Discord converts players.

- Join through the current official invite: <https://discord.gg/indiegamejoe>.
- Read the server and channel rules before posting and confirm the channel name
  still exists.
- Use one strong, current gameplay clip or trailer and the developer's exact
  human-written wording. Include the relevant Steam or demo destination if the
  rules allow it.
- Do not tag or privately message Joe merely to demand attention. Do not repost
  the same game repeatedly unless the current rules or a genuinely new beat
  support it.
- Record a resulting feature, click spike, or wishlist change as project
  evidence; do not promise that submission will produce coverage.

Source: [Indie Game Joe's explanation of how to pitch him](https://www.reddit.com/r/IndieDev/comments/1umjof3/how_to_pitch_your_game_to_me_indie_game_joe/)
and the [public Discord listing](https://discord.com/servers/indie-game-joe-939249783573479464).

### r/Games Indie Sunday

As verified from a June 14, 2026 event hub, Indie Sunday creates a limited
developer self-promotion window with stricter formatting than an ordinary
Reddit post.

- It runs for 24 hours beginning Sunday at 12:00 a.m. Eastern.
- Only developers may submit their own games.
- Use a self-post with the `Indie Sunday` flair and the prescribed
  `Game - developer - short description` title structure.
- Put actual gameplay video in the post body; merely referring to a Steam-page
  trailer is insufficient.
- Do not run a key or game giveaway.
- Do not post the same game or developer more than once every 60 days.

Find and read the current week's hub before preparing or publishing the post;
the current hub is authoritative if these details change. Source:
[r/Games Indie Sunday Hub, June 14, 2026](https://www.reddit.com/r/Games/comments/1u5au1n/indie_sunday_hub_june_14_2026/).

### #PitchYaGame

Treat an official round as a dated pitch opportunity rather than a routine
hashtag. Verify the current round date and rules at
<https://pitchyagame.com/>. Current guidance permits one pitch per game, per
platform, on the official round day. Use the developer's exact pitch and a
strong current gameplay clip; keep the opportunity distinct from an unrelated
announcement unless combining them is deliberate.

### Screenshot Saturday

Use `#ScreenshotSaturday` when a genuinely interesting current image or clip
is ready. It is a recurring community convention and a lightweight cadence
slot, not a major beat or a substitute for reaching the game's player niche.
Confirm that the tag is still active on the selected platform, then compare its
click and wishlist performance with ordinary posts before investing heavily.

## Match the asset to the surface

- Lead with authentic gameplay and make the hook understandable without
  requiring the caption.
- Export native landscape, square, or vertical versions when the surface needs
  them. Do not squeeze one aspect ratio into another.
- Keep readable interface or dialogue on screen long enough to read; otherwise
  suppress it cleanly.
- Cross-posting the same underlying footage is efficient, but adapt framing,
  duration, subtitles, link placement, and metadata to the surface.
- Do not repost the exact same clip repeatedly within one channel merely to
  fill a schedule. Reuse it when it serves a distinct audience or beat and the
  platform rules permit it.

The developer supplies every public caption, title, reply template, and call to
action. The agent may resize or sequence approved assets, faithfully translate
approved text, insert verified fields, and flag factual or rules problems; it
must not draft synthetic social copy.

## Use a sustainable cadence

Build regular posts from real development evidence: a mechanic, historical or
design detail, before-and-after improvement, playtest result, or short narrated
demonstration. Reserve coordinated bursts for announcement, demo, festival,
date-reveal, and release beats.

Do not recommend a daily cadence unless the developer already has a repeatable
capture and editing pipeline and the posts produce useful results. Time spent
making filler competes with finishing the game and preparing stronger beats.

## Publish deliberately and measure

Before each post:

1. Name the audience, purpose, destination, and measurable outcome.
2. Recheck current venue rules and cooldowns.
3. Use a campaign-specific Steam UTM link when external links are allowed.
4. Record the baseline and obtain explicit authorization before publishing.

Afterward, record platform-native reach and retention plus Steam visits,
wishlists, demo starts, sales, useful replies, curator pickup, and coverage at
consistent intervals. A large view count with no qualified traffic is not the
same result as a smaller post that reaches the right players.

Participate honestly in communities, disclose the developer relationship, and
answer genuine questions. Do not automate fake engagement, disguise promotion
as feedback, mass-DM members, evade posting limits, or coordinate votes.
